# Code of conduct

mangame should be a place where people can improve software without being
belittled, excluded or pressured.

## Expected behaviour

- Be specific and kind when reviewing work.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete problems.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, identities and ways of
  communicating.
- Give credit for ideas, code, testing, documentation and artwork.
- Keep private information private.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, sustained disruption and publishing someone else's private
communication are not accepted in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, releases and other spaces
where someone represents the project. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, close interactions, temporarily restrict participation or ban someone
when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[h.aarhus@mailbox.org](mailto:h.aarhus@mailbox.org). Include links or
screenshots where possible. Reports will be handled discreetly; retaliation
against someone who reports a concern is itself a violation.
